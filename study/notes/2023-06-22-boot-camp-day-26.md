## What we learned

Equilibrium is the last building block in this stretch, and he calls it one of the easier ones. He saved it for the end because liquidity, fair value gaps, and order blocks are the pieces people mix up. This video is the definition. The chart work is promised for a later sitting. Equilibrium, he says, is a retracement measure that shows where large money is likely to act again.

The drawing is the swing. He measures a high down to a low, or a low up to a high, and marks the halfway point. That line splits the range. In a downtrend he measures the high to the low, and price that has returned above the halfway mark is the high price, which he treats as the favorable side for a short. His grocery picture is the same idea: buyers want the sale, and sellers want the rich price. In an uptrend he measures the low up to the high. Price that has retraced below the halfway mark is the cheap side, where he says large participants want to buy again. The dear side of that up swing is where he says those buyers would rather wait.

He does not click the halfway line by itself. It is a gauge. The band says the price is on sale, and the reason to act is another tool already taught: an order block, a fair value gap, or a break of structure while price is in that discount. The pairing he likes most is a fair value gap sitting inside the discount.

He also restacks the earlier tools so the new one has a place. A liquidity sweep is the inducement. A break of structure is the first entry, the sign he treats as a shift in structure. If that click is missed, the order block is the move that caused the sweep, a first retracement entry. A fair value gap is the next retracement, an imbalance that gets filled and reacts. Equilibrium tells him whether that gap or block sits at a price large money would actually want. The side he calls premium is the expensive side, and he says a re-entry there is the price participants try to avoid.

## What to remember

- Equilibrium is the halfway point of a swing, used to mark the cheap side and the expensive side.
- In his model, buys sit in the lower half of an up swing, and shorts sit in the upper half of a down swing.
- The halfway line is a location filter. The entry reason is still a sweep, a structure break, a block, or a gap.
- A gap inside the discount is the pairing he singles out.
- He treats this block as the simple one, taught after the tools people confuse.
