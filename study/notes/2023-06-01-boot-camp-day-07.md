## What we learned

Day 7 is a mindset day about staying a separate person from the teacher. He says attachment to a mentor is a poor reason to keep forcing a method that never clicks. If another approach is fully understood and is working, he tells the viewer to use that approach, and to keep the discipline lessons either way. Pieces from his material and pieces from elsewhere can be combined. His claim is that his own method is simple ideas placed in an order, so any subset the student actually understands is usable, and a subset that stays opaque can be left unused. He says many approaches are profitable.

The origin story he gives is personal observation, not a copied system. Sitting through one session on one market, he kept seeing a strong push at the open that immediately reversed, noticed it kept happening, and later read that behavior as a liquidity sweep. He offers that as the reason to study one's own charts instead of trying to become him.

He treats progress as a private comparison. A friend who is already having profitable months is on a different learning curve, and measuring against that friend is, in his view, a way to damage psychology. The life half of the lesson is the same idea off the chart: choose the day's clothes, opinions, and actions without adjusting them to peers. He links that ordinary confidence to the confidence required to hold a chart opinion while many other people are positioned the other way. The assignment is one day of acting on one's own choices, plus a written note on how that day felt, including the discomfort when someone questions it.

## What to remember

- A concept that still does not make sense can be left unused. Pieces that do make sense can be combined.
- His session anecdote is an origin story for liquidity. The definition of liquidity belongs to the liquidity lesson.
- The scoreboard he accepts is one's own earlier work. A friend's pace is a different curve.
- Holding an ordinary opinion without editing it for the room is the confidence he connects to holding a chart read.
- Other profitable approaches existing is part of the lesson. This series is his version of what he knows.
