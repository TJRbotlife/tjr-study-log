## What we learned

Day 9 is a pause, not a chart lesson. He says the first week is over and the second week is when motivation thins out. He treats that fade as normal and says the uploads will keep coming through it.

He frames the slow pace as the point. Liquidity is the piece he calls most essential to the way he trades, so he splits it across several lessons instead of dumping the whole idea in one sitting. He compares the course to studying for a hard exam: one concept at a time, understood before the next one is added.

He also separates finishing the series from becoming profitable. He says the two months of lessons will not, by themselves, make someone profitable. What happens with the material after the series is what he thinks decides that. He treats a small share of a large audience as a good outcome, and he says even that outcome sits later than the end of the uploads.

The only assignment is rest. He says daily chart work burns people out, invites over-analysis, and rattles judgment. He wants at least one day a week away from the work, and he uses this check-in as that day. He says he expects similar check-ins about every other week.
