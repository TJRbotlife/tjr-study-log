With the S&P partial already booked, a stop on gold would leave the day near break even or at a small loss in his telling, and a target would leave a large gain. On the daily he sees gold inside accumulation, so a later sweep of highs would make sense, and he still wants more downside inside the weekly downtrend. An hourly break could be a retrace or a real change of direction.

He names the loss he is willing to take. Confluences in line with the bias, a workable reward, a clean execution, and then price simply turns: that is the loss he wants. A bad stop or a rushed entry is the result he wants kept out of the book. The takeaway he gives himself on gold is confidence. Highs swept, structure turned down, the gap filled, and a close back under the gap should have been the short, stops above, with an order block as the first target and a better reward than the later entry he actually took. He still found a second entry inside the same idea, and he says that idea can still be invalidated by a leg up. Unemployment claims that day were bearish in his read, and the pairs he was watching were already bearish, so the shorts matched both.
