## What we learned

Day 1 is the contract for the series, not a setup. He says the viewer will not learn a strategy in this video. The work he asks for is rewatching lessons, doing the homework, spending about an hour a day on the charts, and keeping a journal.

He treats "get rich this week" and thumbnail claims of a perfect win rate as the wrong frame. The horizon he states is closer to one or two years. He also describes an early personal habit of fading a sweep of a prior high with a tight stop. He presents that as his own start, names liquidity sweeps, and points at later teaching. It is not a finished rule in this video.
