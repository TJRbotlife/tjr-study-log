## What we learned

Day 3 is a mindset day. He says the series will alternate chart lessons and discipline lessons because a course that files psychology into one short section leaves the harder part untouched. He claims the chart ideas can be the same for viewer and teacher, and that the gap he sees is mindset: bailing out, or failing to take the trade the plan already describes.

The foundation he asks for is one daily habit that feels uncomfortable. A habit that is already easy does not count. The point of the discomfort is practice at doing the thing after the desire to do it has faded. He cites a figure he remembers as about 21 days before a habit forms, and about 60 days before the same act feels routine. Motivation, he says, is strongest at the start and usually gone after about a week. What remains on the day after that week is discipline.

He also ties the habit to a picture of the person someone intends to become, then asks what that person already does each day, and copies that. Professional trading, in his examples, looks like a plan, controlled risk, and even emotion. The growth rate he names is small and daily. Later mindset days are when he expects to ask whether the habit is still in place. A public diary of the effort is optional; the required piece is the one hard habit, started the day of the video.
