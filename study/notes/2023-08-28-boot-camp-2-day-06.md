## What we learned

This is a short second video from the same day, recorded because he took an index trade after the gold trade and the first target had just been hit. He says the picture resembled gold.

Price took liquidity from four-hour lows, broke structure higher, and extended out of an accumulation area he had marked. The fair value gap above that, and a smaller accumulation inside the move, did not get filled. Instead price swept those lows and rallied again. He treats the first extension, and a shallow retest, as not enough. What he wanted was another sweep of the lows, then a retest of the accumulation, which he also calls the order block that sat after the sweep.

He scaled to the one-minute. A break of structure there was enough for him to enter. The stop went under those lows. The first target was a nearby set of highs, with more room above if price wanted it. He does not assume a rally all the way to the far highs. By the time he recorded, he had taken half off and moved the stop to break-even, so he calls the remainder free of the original risk. The session was moving into Asian premarket. He expects price to move, and he does not expect a move large enough to matter over those hours. His alerts sat at break-even and at the second target, toward the accumulation that preceded an earlier large drop.

He calls the day two wins. Gold, the trade from the earlier video, reached three targets and then stopped back at break-even. This index trade had just reached its first target, with the second still open.

- The index idea followed the same story as gold: four-hour liquidity, a break higher, then a sweep that skipped the gap.
- An unfilled fair value gap did not cancel the idea once the lows were swept and structure broke again.
- The first pop out of accumulation was not his entry. The later retest after another sweep was.
- The one-minute break was the trigger, the nearby highs were the first target, and the far highs were optional.
- After half came off, he treated the rest as no longer carrying the original risk.
- He was not looking for a meaningful move through the Asian premarket hours, only alerts at break-even and the next target.
