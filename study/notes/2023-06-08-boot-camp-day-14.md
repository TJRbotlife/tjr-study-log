## What we learned

Day 14 introduces fair value gaps by definition only. He treats the name as interchangeable with liquidity void and imbalance, and he says this video explains what they are and why he uses them. How to mark them on a chart is postponed so the idea can settle.

He sets the gap as the opposite of the liquidity already covered. A liquidity pool is where resting orders sit and where he says the market will reach to transact. A gap is a price range with no contradicting orders. A fast rally, in this telling, exists because sell orders were absent through that range. A fast dump with no pause exists because resting buy orders were absent. If those orders had been there, price would have hesitated on the way through.

Inside a trend of higher highs and higher lows, he expects pullbacks to reach back into those empty ranges. Once price is there, he says larger players can place orders into a vacuum and the market can leave in their direction. His own use is specific. He does not treat the gap as his main entry. Liquidity sweeps tell him where the market is trying to go. The gap tells him where a retracement may be drawn, or where a second entry might sit. What makes that useful to him is a reaction, often a break of structure on a lower timeframe after a larger timeframe gap has been filled.

He says the same pieces apply on any timeframe and any market, because he believes this is how price moves. Adding later tools, he says, only adds more signs that orders were filled. This day has no chart homework. He asks for something productive and a little uncomfortable after the video, and he repeats that constant work without a break leads to burnout.

## What to remember

- A fair value gap, a liquidity void, and an imbalance are one idea in this lesson: a range that moved because the other side was missing.
- He expects trends to retrace into those empty ranges, because that is where he says new orders can be placed into a vacuum.
- Sweeps are his direction tool. Gaps are his retracement tool, or a second place to participate after direction is known.
- A filled gap becomes interesting to him when a lower timeframe then shows a structural reaction.
- He presents the idea as independent of one market or one timeframe, and he is willing to ignore a tool he does not yet understand.
