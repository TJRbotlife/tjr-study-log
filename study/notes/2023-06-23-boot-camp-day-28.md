## What we learned

Day 28 puts the halfway line on charts. He opens with the same definition: equilibrium measures a swing so the midpoint can show a premium and a discount for what he calls smart money. On an up swing, the half above the midpoint is premium, and the half back toward the low is the discount where he says those buyers want price. On a down swing he measures high to low. The return into the upper half is where he looks for shorts, because that is the rich price, the side he called favorable for selling in the definition lesson. The midpoint by itself stays a weak reason to click. He wants a fair value gap or an order block in that band, then a structure break as confirmation.

The tool settings are part of the lesson. He uses a Gann box or a Fibonacci and keeps only the levels zero, one-half, and one. The extra Fibonacci marks come off. If the first swing's midpoint is never touched, he drags the measure to the newer high or the newer low and draws it again.

On the charts he stacks the line with the other blocks. A push that breaks structure, runs through the midpoint, fills a gap, and takes resting liquidity is the shape he clicks through, with prior liquidity as the destinations he marks. When the midpoint is hit and he cannot find a gap or a block on the daily or the four hour, he says a structure break by itself is thin confirmation. He treats the same halfway idea as the reason people already like the fifty level, and a nearby Fibonacci retracement, on every timeframe. The working method in the examples is to scale down: a higher-timeframe discount, then a lower-timeframe structure break, order block, or gap.

One past position he walks was a short from a daily midpoint inside a larger downtrend. The higher-timeframe trend was not an uptrend, so he says he followed what that market was showing. He scaled to the four hour, saw a large imbalance filled, scaled to the one hour, took the structure break, placed the stop beyond the extreme, and aimed at a series of prior liquidity pools. In the lesson that example reached three of four targets. He presents it as the pieces working together. The midpoint locates the area, and the other tools time the click. He says the remaining skill is how reliably someone can see that stack.

## What to remember

- The drawing is zero, one-half, and one, taken from the swing that is still in force. A new extreme means he redraws.
- Discount for a buy is the lower half of an up swing. The short zone in these examples is the upper half of a down swing.
- Midpoint plus a structure break, with no block and no gap, is the thin version he sets aside.
- In the chart examples, the higher-timeframe midpoint is the area, and the lower timeframe supplies the confirming break, block, or gap.
- Prior liquidity is the destination he marks once that stack is in place.
